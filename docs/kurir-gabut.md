# Kurir Gabut

Approved scope: a small, peaceful delivery game inspired by the handmade feel of Messenger, with original characters and environment.

- One procedural low-poly village; five deliveries, automatic pickup/drop-off near residents.
- Third-person perspective from behind the courier at shoulder height, with wall obstruction detection. W/S walk, A/D turn; equivalent arrow and touch buttons. Tap a street for automatic route following.
- Connected main streets and narrow alleys between houses; movement stays on paved corridors. Camera-relative destination compass.
- Localized objective and progress HUD. No accounts, network gameplay, saved progress, or external assets.
- Each delivery adds a village reaction: lights, pennants, dancing, flowers, and a following cat.
- Shared Three.js dependency, capped pixel ratio, no real-time shadows or post-processing.
- Simulation separate from rendering; collision, delivery order, completion, cleanup, and localization tested.
- Exclude the longer experience from the existing 45-second Daily lineup.
- Verify production build, real browser play, mobile layout, pause/resume and restart. Asset budget is a target, measured after building.
